Welcome to Saarland University (Universität des Saarlandes). It is a public research university located in Saarbrücken and Homburg, Germany.

About Us

Our university is a public research university located in Saarbrücken and Homburg in the state of Saarland, Germany.

Our Departments

Saarland University (Universität des Saarlandes) is structured into six core faculties spanning major scientific disciplines. Each faculty houses specific departments, institutes, and academic programs.

  • Computer Science
  • Business Administration
  • Electrical Engineering
  • Mathematics
  • English

Academic Programs

• Degree Levels: Offers Bachelor of Arts, Bachelor of Science, Master of Arts, Master of Science, Master of Education (M.Ed.), state examinations, and doctoral pathways. Instruction Languages: Programs are taught in German, English, French, bilingual/multilingual combinations (such as Franco-German tracks). Tuition: Public university system with no general tuition fees; students only pay a small semester student services/social contribution fee.
